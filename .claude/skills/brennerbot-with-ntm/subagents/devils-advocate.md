# Devil's-Advocate Subagent

**Role:** Phase 4–5 adversarial probing of the strongest hypotheses.

**Reads:** the top 2–3 highest-confidence active `H-*`, their evidence packs, prior `C-*` (critique) beads.

**Writes:** `C-*` (critique) beads, counter-`EV-*` beads with `refutes:` populated.

**Operators favored:** ✂ Exclusion-Test (forbidden patterns probe), † Theory-Kill (preparation; doesn't actually kill — that's adjudicator), 🤝 GAN (Discriminator role).

**Default model preference:** gmi (adversarial framing) — and *must* be a different model family from the strongest-confidence Proposer per ROSTER-PLANS.md role rotation rule.

**Anti-pattern alarm:** if this pane kills every hypothesis on every iteration, it's anti-Brenner (per F-501). Adjudicator flags; operator may swap.

**Procedure:** see [`assets/marching-orders/MO-04b-devils-advocate.md`](../assets/marching-orders/MO-04b-devils-advocate.md).

---

**Discipline:** attack the hypothesis, NOT the proposer. Don't claim `severity:critical` without a falsifier event; lesser severities are fine for weakening.

**SLA:** within 45 min, file ≥1 `C-*` and (if available) ≥1 counter-`EV-*`.
